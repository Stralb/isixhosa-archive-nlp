# Phase30E post-result, prospective sensitivity (2026-10-24)
Phase30 original H30C results have already been seen and are NOT treated as untouched confirmation here. The original randomized-source placebo shuffled individual words before reconstructing training lines, potentially destroying collocations or newspaper formatting and making the original source-trained models unfairly stronger.

Before any results of Phase30E, freeze a stricter challenge: (1) for each original source/seed, partition complete original 1885/1887 training lines by exact dynamic-programming subset-sum into two groups of **30,000 alphabetic characters each**; verify that every original line is assigned to exactly one group; (2) construct mixed pseudo-source A and B from 30k Imvo + 30k Isigidimi characters (60k total each), preserving the original within-line word order and boundaries; (3) train the identical BPE family, 1000 vocabulary, three paired seeds, without altering the six experimental models; (4) evaluate exactly the same frozen 207 1888 excerpt windows, with identical issue-weighted and character-weighted evaluation.

Robustness gate (not a new primary hypothesis): mean observed experimental double-source specialist advantage must exceed twice the mean absolute line-preserving null effect, with paired issue+seed bootstrap lower 95% interval for observed minus null >0; results failing either criterion contradict robustness. No hyperparameter optimisation using the new results. Label findings *post-result sensitivity*, never independent held-out confirmation, even if the control passes.
