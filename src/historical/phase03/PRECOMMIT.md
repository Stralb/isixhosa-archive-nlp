# Phase 3 precommit: competitive inference under archive loss

Status at freeze: BEFORE ANY PHASE3 YEAR-PAIR SCORES OR SELECTION MASKS.
Date: 2026-10-24. This is a separate publication-oriented programme, not Assignment 2.

## Primary scientific correction
Phase2 used identical page-level lexical outcomes in pseudo-period A/B for all non-injected word forms. Matching the exact same page across periods trivially yields zero for null forms. A dedicated Phase3 paired-page audit quantifies this leakage, and the competitive benchmark below uses DISJOINT actual publication years and issues. We will not call the Phase2 outcome-selection failures proof of a novel algorithm.

## New archive-complete finite-population estimand
The target is the difference in uniform page means of Y= min(occurrences/alpha-word-count, 0.02), for each of 80 lexical forms, between two adjacent/differently dated digitized XhTxt year corpora. This is the difference in the *digitized archive's* complete eligible page inventory, not a historical population prevalence, not word sense, not culture.

Development: actual pages from 1884 versus 1885. Held-out test: 1887 versus 1888; these years share NO issues with development. Only complete Imvo four-page and Isigidimi eight-page issues are included; omit 1887 incomplete Imvo issues. Archive membership, page positions, published date, source, issue ID and original alpha-token counts are known in the optimistic full frame; lexical outcomes for masked pages are hidden from estimators. Raw text stays in the private oracle only.

For computational comparability choose 80 alphabetic wordforms from the highest corpus-wide 1884-1885 frequencies, with lexical tie breaking; freeze that list BEFORE extracting year-1887/88 counts. Predeclare injected word-form indices: DOWN=(6,8,10,12,14,16), UP=(18,24,30,36,42,48). NATURAL world uses the genuine digitized text from both years. INJECTED world modifies only the later-year counts using binomial removal of 40% of each DOWN word's tokens, then transfers random background counts into the six UP forms, with background-pool Bernoulli probability 0.0051 distributed multinomially. Every modified page retains its token total. This is lexical-rate intervention, NOT semantic ground truth. All 80 forms may have real year-to-year differences even in NATURAL world: never call non-injected forms exact nulls.

Selection masks are drawn independently across years except for within-issue linked missingness in ISSUE_DROPOUT. Six preregistered scenarios: MAR_SOURCE_PAGE, MAR_LENGTH_DATE, MNAR_LATENT (retention depends on actually hidden text's first-four-feature rate, separately per period), ISSUE_DROPOUT, ZERO_CELL (Isigidimi back pages in B entirely absent), and REPEAT_PREF (retention depends on across-issue repeated 13-token fragments from the private full text). The full archive-known frame is an unrealistically favorable assumption. Dev 80 repetitions/scenario/world, test 120; scenario RNG keyed by phase/period/scenario/replicate/world; two different world masks when outcome-dependent. No page matching across years is possible.

## Methods already in statistical literature
Point: POOLED; SOURCE_HALF_STD using target-period true cell weights, unavailable on missing cells; EST_IPW logistic propensity from visible source/half/quarter/log length, clipped to [0.08,.98]; RIDGE_IMPUTE with same observed covariates; AIPW with ridge outcome regression and fitted propensity (doubly robust only given MAR, positivity, appropriate models); and KNOWN_HT_ORACLE using true selection probabilities, unavailable in practice. Under structural positivity loss, oracle HT and source-standardized estimator must abstain. Estimated logistic propensity may output numbers but is extrapolating under MNAR / positivity failure.

Partial-ID intervals: unconstrained exact Manski bounds for Y in [0,.02], and source-half pattern-mixture bounds assuming the unobserved-minus-observed mean per period/cell is within gamma in {0.0005, 0.0020}, falling back to [0,.02] in wholly absent cells. Report the fraction of simulation truths satisfying each gamma *as an oracle diagnostic*, never infer assumption validity from masked data. Tipping point report minimal gamma grid {0, 0.00025, 0.0005, 0.001, 0.002, 0.004, 0.01} at which the interval includes zero. Any positive assertion conditional on gamma must name that assumption.

Interval inference: an explicit finite-population selection-design 95% Wald CI for oracle HT in positive-probability scenarios, using the correct Bernoulli independent-page variance or issue-level Bernoulli cluster variance for issue dropout. Evaluate empirical interval inclusion over repeated selection masks, CONDITIONAL ON EACH FIXED DIGITIZED ARCHIVE, not 95% cultural or superpopulation coverage. Source-cell standardized and fitted-IPW means also receive cluster-issue influence-function Wald intervals, which are approximations and for fitted IPW omit propensity estimation uncertainty; report their calibration. Manski intervals are exact *identification sets* for the finite population and are NOT 95% confidence intervals. Imbens-Manski confidence corrections for random-sample estimated bounds are not directly applicable when the observed finite-page outcomes and the full frame are treated as exact; do not invent such CIs.

## Metrics and thresholds frozen in advance
- Mean absolute error against known complete-archive truth times 10,000.
- Primary practical sign classification uses threshold abs(estimate)>0.00075 and true abs(effect)>0.00020; calls when true magnitude<=0.00020 are counted as unjustified practical decisions, while wrong-direction decisions on larger truths are errors. No p-value interpretations.
- All interval sign calls require bounds exclude zero. Compare proportion of decisions, wrong-call risk conditional on deciding, and true-effect containment; true-effect containment for identification sets is not statistical coverage.
- Report mean 95% CI inclusion among repeated simulated masks for oracle HT, source standardization and estimated IPW, separately by scenario/year/world. Do not claim validity for fitted models under MNAR.
- To compare abstention fairly, point methods are additionally ranked by absolute estimated effect and evaluated at fixed 10%,20%,30% decision coverage within each scenario/world/replicate, with wrong-call risk including near-zero targets. Bounds cannot be forced to decide when they include zero.
- Primary cross-scenario decision gate: a new algorithm is not justified unless these strong standard baselines show a material remaining problem at equal decision coverage, AND there is independent information or a defensible assumption that could overcome it. A known impossibility under arbitrary MNAR is not a method gap.

## Controls and caveats
The Phase2 test has ALREADY been examined; do not retune any Phase3 choice against it. The new 1887/1888 corpus was previously explored in a separate tokenization programme, so this is new phase-specific masks/targets, NOT an untouched external corpus or independent historical source. Not all written words are human-verified isiXhosa; XhTxt denotes the archive's language view. No semantic or cultural inference, no genre labels. Only pre-registered masks, fixed word screening from development, no neural modelling needed for this statistical benchmark. Negative results and missed assumptions are mandatory. No paper drafted.
