## Exact-text overlap — not a clean-room claim

Issue-date train/test separation is clean, but **514 / 2,493 (20.6%) held-out page-view excerpts contain at least one exact 13-word training-year sequence**. The corresponding fractions are 35/134 (26.1%) for 1888 English view and 369/927 (39.8%) for 1889–94 English view. This is newspaper-content repetition across different issue dates, not proof that training used test-period pages. A post-hoc test excluding any such exact training 13-gram retained **99 English-view 1888 excerpts**, on which the BPE English bigram allocation effect remained **−0.0287385 BPC** versus the full-set **−0.0372167 BPC**. This sensitivity is selective and cannot replace the prespecified full-set result; shorter/paraphrased duplication may remain.

## Stability and counterexample

- BPE 1888 English-view allocation, leave-one-issue-out: **[−1.89560, −1.86224]** pieces per 100 characters; consistently negative.
- BPE 1888 English-view bigram allocation, leave-one-issue-out: **[−0.038203, −0.036306]** BPC; consistently negative.
- BPE English-view bigram allocation, leave-one-year-out during 1889–94: **[−0.026927, −0.023746]** BPC; consistently negative.
- Later 1889–94 **whole policy** WW−PP English bigram: **+0.0045033 BPC** with issue bootstrap **[+0.001758,+0.007283]**, but **six-year cluster bootstrap [−0.002682,+0.013050] crosses zero** and **3 of 5** seeds are positive. The appropriate conclusion is that changing the whole policy was **not consistently beneficial**, **not** systematic significant worsening across independent years.

## Scientific interpretation and limits

Changing training-data sampling distributions in this one *Imvo Zabantsundu* newspaper corpus changes the segmentation-efficiency trade-off between the archive's \`EnTxt\` and \`XhTxt\` views; a narrower English-view benefit transfers to a simple smoothed count-bigram language model. This does **not** prove effects on authentic cultural attitudes, sentiment, societal fairness, modern isiXhosa, neural networks, Transformers, or an independently tested population of archives.

The archive view labels are **not expert-adjudicated language ground truth**; five tokenizer seeds are technical repeats, not independent newspapers; issue bootstraps condition on observed publication issues. \`alpha=100\` was the best of three frozen values, **not** exhaustive smoothing tuning. Re-use of historical test years in earlier exploration is an unavoidable limitation and must be stated.
