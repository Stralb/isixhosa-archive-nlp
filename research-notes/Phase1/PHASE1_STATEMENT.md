# Research statement for the handoff

**Question.** How do English/isiXhosa-view allocation and within-view page selection independently contribute to historical newspaper tokenizer efficiency, and is any advantage robust to repeated text and downstream prediction?

**Completed.** The Phase-1 data and methods audit confirms the original study's code/results, separates training and validation issues, identifies repeated-text risk, algebraically identifies all four factorial cells, implements deterministic samplers, and independently checks their source-only behavior.

**Not completed.** The four new tokenizer evaluations, repeated-text reruns, new downstream results, human genre labels, final two-page abstract and submission ethics materials.

**Important interpretation.** The factorial experiment is a follow-up motivated by published earlier-phase effects: it tests a more specific mechanistic explanation, but the later-year archive cannot be called untouched. Source-specific segmentation disparities alone are not proven social harms.
