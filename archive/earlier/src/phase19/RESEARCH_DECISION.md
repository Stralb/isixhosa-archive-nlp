# Phase 19 — Research decision, not a paper

**Frozen question:** Does Phase 18's language-gated 15 raw + 5 English candidate enrichment improve source-anchored retrieval when searches are nonverbatim historical keyword questions?

**Design and safeguards:** 55 English searches composed before any new retrieval scores were evaluated (40 Imvo, 15 Isigidimi); no exact three-token query phrases copied from source paragraphs; one unique issue per query; 55 issues disjoint from all Phase 18 sampled English source issues. Full 3,608-page raw index and original paired archive-field views. Same BM25 formula and fuzzy partial-ratio reranker as Phase 18. Source-grouped uncertainty. A source page was known by composition; repeated 13-word within-paragraph evidence spans defined additional gold pages. This is NOT independent human semantic relevance annotation.

**Frozen results (equal-source macro, Top-1 known evidence):** raw BM25 **50.42%**; raw BM25 + fuzzy **27.92%**; gated **27.92%**; unconditional augmentation **27.92%**; hard language routing **36.25%**; English-only field + fuzzy **36.25%**; raw+English RRF + fuzzy **36.25%**. Candidate recall@20: raw **96.25%**, gated **96.25%**. Gated minus raw BM25: **-22.50 pp**, issue-bootstrap 95% CI **[-37.92,-7.92] pp**. Gated minus raw-fuzzy: **0.00 pp**, interval **[0,0]**. Gated source-specific effects vs plain BM25: Imvo **-5.00 pp** (40 examples); Isigidimi **-40.00 pp** (15 examples).

**H19a: REJECTED in this source-anchored pilot.** Gated augmentation fails its prospective +3 pp improvement criterion against BOTH unfuzzied BM25 and fuzzy BM25. There is no evidence here for a novel or reliable general-purpose method.

**Retrospective diagnostics (NOT confirmatory):** The frozen short-query archive-view classifier selected **English on all 55 queries**, so gated and unconditional policies were identical on this set. Gated candidate membership changed in **32/55** queries, but added **zero** newly annotated relevant pages and removed **zero**. Raw BM25 already retrieved an eligible evidence page somewhere in its top20 for 96.25% of queries. Full-page fuzzy reranking overturned **13** previously correct plain-BM25 Top-1 outcomes (7 Imvo, 6 Isigidimi), but repaired only **5** plain-BM25 Top-1 misses (all Imvo). In the Isigidimi subset, fuzzy top1 returned an Imvo page **8/15** times. This indicates sensitivity to reranking/query form; we have not causally isolated which specific substring match is misleading.

**Major boundaries:** Queries are **assistant-composed with visible source paragraphs**, not external users' independently formulated information needs. Known-source and same-excerpt reprint gold is incomplete for semantic alternatives, so wrong-page predictions may still be topically relevant. Imvo examples overwhelmingly come from page 3 editorial material and Isigidimi often from notices/advertising; cross-newspaper comparisons confound source, year, genre and sample size. No isiXhosa expert or independently annotated isiXhosa intent. No aligned real historical OCR scans. Character 3–5-gram TF-IDF baseline was **attempted but exceeded a 250-second execution budget** before yielding any result; it was explicitly omitted, not replaced or silently treated as failed. No pretrained multilingual dense/hybrid benchmark was run.

**Decision:** Freeze Phase 18 unchanged; do NOT generalize its +5.3 pp synthetic-noise advantage to nonverbatim search. Accept Phase 19 as a **negative-transfer result with a diagnostic ranker failure**. Do not derive a new method claim or write an abstract.

**Next genuinely necessary experiment:** Before proposing a retrieval method, create an independently authored *information-need* benchmark with multiple independently checked answer-bearing pages. Recruit readers to write English historical information needs without seeing the indexed query passages; source adjudication can focus on English excerpts first. For isiXhosa-language intent, obtain qualified linguistic review rather than assigning labels. Precommit issue splits and candidate budgets, and compare plain BM25, fuzzy, gated and unconditional variants, field fusion, and a strong multilingual dense/hybrid baseline. Evaluate query intent and authentic OCR evidence separately, not as synthetic replications. If a method fails plain BM25 or only wins on generated source-derived snippets, reject general retrieval-method novelty and consider an evaluation/resource contribution instead.
