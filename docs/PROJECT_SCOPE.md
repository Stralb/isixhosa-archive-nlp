# Research scope and publication status

This repository documents controlled NLP experiments on training-data sampling, tokenisation and language modelling with historical isiXhosa newspaper material. The main coursework investigation uses a two-factor sampling design and reports both positive and negative findings.

The original newspaper transcriptions are not redistributed here because redistribution rights have not been established. Research outputs and methods are not evidence of changes in historical language meaning or of social unfairness.

The complete coursework submission includes a separate two-page extended abstract and required ethics and contribution declarations. Those deliverables are not replaced by a public code repository.
