# Research progression and scope

The overall investigation is broader than the final coursework factorial experiment. For review purposes, we preserve the progression without presenting every stage as an independent confirmatory result.

| Research track | Work recorded | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original studies, Phases 1 to 18 | Dataset audit, historical newspaper comparisons, BPE/Unigram mechanism, downstream models, explorations and early replication | Scientific development; parts superseded by later design |
| Original studies, Phases 19 to 31 | Retrieval controls, word-form analysis, lexical/contextual tests, historical transfer and robustness | Exploratory or conditional studies with their own gates |
| Original studies, Phases 32 to 33 | Archive language-view visibility and sampling exposure interventions | Direct precursor to the coursework factorial |
| Excellence Sprint, Phases 1 to 6 | Frozen 2 by 2 factorial, 40 fits, held-out evaluation, repeat tests, count-bigram downstream study, adversarial audit | **Main coursework result** |
| Historical Inference, Phases 1 to 4 | Archival selection and partial-identification benchmarks, strong baseline comparison, hypothetical page recovery | Separate methodological programme with valuable negative findings |
| Historical Inference, Phase 5 | Search for authentic missing page evidence | No verified new text recovered in available record |

Historical Inference is kept separate so its simulated lexical-change bounds are not mistaken for the tokenizer BPC experiment. Different programmes report pieces per 100 alphabetic characters, bits per character, counts per 10,000 words and selective sign-error rates. These metrics are not interchangeable.
