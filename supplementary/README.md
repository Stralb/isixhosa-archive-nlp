# Earlier studies and separate historical-inference research

The primary coursework experiment is under [`coursework/isiXhosa_Excellence_Sprint`](../coursework/isiXhosa_Excellence_Sprint). The directories here preserve source scripts, decision records, selected summary tables and figures from **earlier research releases**. They are included to show the origin of the experimental decisions, rejected ideas and broader research investigation.

- `early-research/`: source code from cumulative Phases 1 to 18 where available in the retained portable archive.
- `original-research/`: Phases 19 to 33, including retrieval tests, word-form analyses, text visibility and the sampling precursor.
- `historical-inference/Historical_Inference_Programme/`: separate Phases 1 to 4 studying missing archival text and partial identification.

These snapshots are curated, not byte-complete historical release archives. The full original releases included raw or derived text whose public redistribution is not yet cleared and some large numerical arrays. **All Python and shell scripts present in the retained releases were copied where safe**, along with reviewer-oriented evidence. Preserve frozen versions when comparing to the original release ZIPs.

## Research status

The earlier projects contain experiments that failed, were superseded, or were designed after viewing some of the same evaluation years. They are not thirty-three independent confirmations of the main coursework conclusion.

The historical-inference programme found a benchmark shortcut in Phase 2 and documented its correction. Phase 3 found that existing estimation methods can transfer poorly under hidden-text-dependent missingness. Phase 4 found that simulated recovery of missing pages often leaves historical lexical-change directions unidentified. It did **not** recover genuine missing historical text, establish a new algorithm, or verify historic semantic change.

Separate historical-inference Phase 5 archival acquisition remained unfinished as of the handoff; no recovered archival page text is included. See [the main handoff](../docs/RESEARCH_HISTORY.md) for research boundaries.
